# Study-Spots-UW

UX research and prototype for a UW study-spot finder app: filter nearby cafes, libraries, and outdoor spaces by hours, Wi-Fi, outlets, noise level, and cost, with an interactive map. Built with Lovable.

**[Interactive prototype](https://uw-study-buddy.lovable.app/)** · **[Research deck (Google Slides)](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1aOuedp2F7Np_Qb4821HMIQmZhLzSsJ2jOEZ6q378Rfg/edit?slide=id.p#slide=id.p)**

## Problem

Students who want to study off campus have no reliable way to know whether a space fits their habits and preferences. Seat availability, Wi-Fi and outlet access, cost requirements, and ambiance are all unknown until they show up. This leads to inefficient searching and wasted trips to spaces that turn out to be unsuitable.

## Causes

- Limited open spaces on campus
- Restricted on-campus operating hours
- No central source of real-time information
- High noise, unreliable Wi-Fi, or poor climate in some spaces
- Poor geographic access to on-campus spaces

## Consequences

- Higher stress from searching for a spot
- Poor concentration in chaotic environments
- Loss of motivation from settling for an uncomfortable space
- Physical discomfort (e.g., studying on a bed instead of at a desk)
- Time wasted finding a space

## Proposed Solution

An app that surfaces key details upfront so students can decide where to go based on their needs.

**Core features**
- **Open toggle:** show only spots that are currently open
- **Amenity filters:** filter by Wi-Fi, outlet access, noise level, and whether a purchase is required
- **Interactive map view:** see exact locations relative to campus alongside the list of spaces
